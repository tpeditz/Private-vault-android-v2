Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google account?

No. Local vault features work without Google sign-in. Connecting Google Drive is optional and used for backup and restore.

Where are my vault records stored?

Records are encrypted on your device. If you choose to export or upload a backup, that creates a separate encrypted copy.

Can I use Private Vault offline?

Yes. Local vault access does not require internet. Online services such as update checks and cloud backups require a connection.

How do I update the app?

Use the in-app update checker or the official GitHub releases page. Keep a verified backup before updating.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Keep your vault and backup passwords somewhere safe. Losing a required password can prevent recovery of encrypted information.
