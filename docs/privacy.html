Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Private Vault is an Android app by Tp Editz. It is designed to store your vault on your device, with optional encrypted backup to a Google account you choose. Google sign-in is not required to use local vault features.

1. Information stored in your vault

The app stores information you choose to add, including passwords, notes, photos, videos, documents, bookmarks, folders and authenticator entries. Vault content is encrypted on your device. Settings, account status and information needed to operate the app may also be stored locally. Your vault password is used to unlock the vault; it is not your Google account password.

2. Google account and Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, the app uses Google sign-in and requests access to your basic account identity, including your email address, and permission to manage its own app data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Google supplies authorization information so the app can perform backup and restore operations. The app uses this access to:

  • Identify the connected account and display its connection status.
  • Upload encrypted vault snapshots when you request a backup or enable automatic backup.
  • List, download, restore and delete the app's own backup snapshots.

Backup encryption occurs on your device before upload. Your backups are stored in your chosen Google Drive account, not on a server operated by Tp Editz. The app does not request access to unrelated Drive files. Google authorization does not itself unlock your encrypted vault.

3. How information is used and shared

Private Vault uses Google account information for the backup and restore features described above. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is limited to the disclosed app features and follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google processes sign-in and Drive storage under its Privacy Policy. When you check for app updates or visit this website, GitHub handles the corresponding network requests under its Privacy Statement. Private Vault also supports user-initiated sharing and opening external links; information shared with a destination you choose is then handled by that destination.

Remote application services (V2.1.0 and later)

Private Vault uses Supabase to retrieve update policies, announcements and remote application settings. These requests may generate standard connection and diagnostic information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps and response status. The app determines rollout eligibility on the device. The remote policy service does not receive your vault password, Google account details, encryption keys, passwords, notes, photos, documents or backup contents through these features. You can continue using local vault features offline.

Supabase hosts the remote service and may retain technical logs in accordance with service configuration. For more information, see Supabase's Privacy Policy. Feedback submission is currently disabled.

Administrator access

The separate administrative control center uses Supabase authentication and multi-factor authentication for authorized administrators. Account and session information and administrative activity records are used to operate and secure the service. Ordinary vault users do not need an administrative account.

4. Device permissions

File access is used to import or export items you select. Optional biometrics use Android's authentication services; the app does not collect raw fingerprint or facial data. You may manage these features in the app and Android settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local vault records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's local data. Backups that you export or upload are separate copies. You can manage and delete cloud snapshots from the app's cloud backup screen. The app is designed to retain up to three recent cloud snapshots, subject to successful cleanup and your Google Drive account.

Disconnecting Google Drive turns off automatic backup and attempts to revoke the app's authorization. It does not automatically erase copies already stored in Google Drive. You can also revoke access through your Google Account connections. To remove existing cloud copies, delete the snapshots before disconnecting or manage the app's stored data through Google Drive.

6. Security and your choices

The app uses local encryption and HTTPS for its network requests. No storage or backup system can guarantee perfect security. Keep your vault and recovery passwords safe, and maintain an independent verified backup of important information. You can use local features without connecting Google, disable automatic backup, and manage device permissions.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions or assistance, contact Tp Editz on Telegram (@Tp_Editz) or Instagram (@tp_ediitz). Avoid sharing vault passwords or private vault content when requesting support. This policy may be updated as app features change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
